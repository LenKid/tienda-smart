# Tienda Smart TS App Logo Design

## Goal

Create a polished SVG logo for the Tienda Smart app icon. The approved visual direction is the **entrelazado** monogram: a compact TS symbol on a square dark background.

## Approved Visual System

- Canvas: 512 × 512 viewBox.
- Outer background: dark `#111827`, matching a premium and high-contrast app icon.
- Blue T: `#208AEF`, aligned with the app splash-screen blue.
- White S: `#FFFFFF`, providing maximum contrast and legibility.
- Smart accent: small green dot `#4ADE80`.
- Corner radius: large enough for a modern app icon while preserving content.
- The T and S must overlap or interlock as a compact monogram rather than appearing as separate text.

## Geometry

- The icon should avoid thin strokes that disappear at 32–48 px.
- The T is the main top/left structural shape.
- The S is the secondary white curved form.
- The green dot is a small visual accent, not a functional control.
- Keep at least 48 px of safe space between the monogram and the square canvas edge.
- Use simple SVG paths and shapes only, with no external fonts or raster assets.

## Deliverable

- Primary file: `assets/images/tienda-smart-logo.svg`.
- The SVG is suitable for direct viewing and future conversion to app icon assets.
- The final deliverable may use a dark rounded-square background and must remain recognizable on transparent or light app launch surfaces.

## Validation

- Open the SVG at 512, 128, 64, and 32 px.
- Confirm the T and S remain distinguishable.
- Confirm the green accent remains visible but does not dominate.
